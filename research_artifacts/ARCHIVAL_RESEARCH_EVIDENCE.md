# Archival research evidence

The authoritative raw evidence is already present under `research_evaluation/`
and is preserved in place. The latest correction ZIP and SHA-256 sidecar are:

- `research_evaluation/chaosflow_research_final_resource_aware_completion_20261116_r2_scientific_classification_correction/ChaosFlow_RESEARCH_R2_SCIENTIFIC_CLASSIFICATION_CORRECTION_Evidence.zip`
- The matching `.sha256` sidecar in the same directory.

This package intentionally does not create a second full copy of the raw
evidence. The local filesystem has approximately 8.8 GiB free and the evidence
tree is approximately 9.3 GB; duplication would consume storage without adding
preservation value. `EVIDENCE_CATALOG.csv` is the explicit old-path to
authoritative-path mapping. External preservation is not claimed until a
checksum-verified copy is actually uploaded or transferred with authorization.
